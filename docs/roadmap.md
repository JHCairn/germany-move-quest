# Germany Move Quest – Product Roadmap

## Purpose

This roadmap describes the long-term direction of Germany Move Quest.

Rather than acting as a release plan, it identifies the major areas of product investment and how the application is expected to evolve over time.

The detailed implementation work is maintained in the Product Backlog.

---

# Product Maturity

Germany Move Quest is evolving through several stages of maturity.

Each stage builds upon the previous one while continuing to refine and improve earlier capabilities as new insights emerge.

The roadmap is therefore iterative rather than linear.

---

## ✓ Foundation

The core architecture and product model have been established.

Completed capabilities include:

* Product Vision
* Product Principles
* Solution Design
* Catalog-driven architecture
* User facts and derived business logic
* Journey model
* Quest system
* Über mich (About Me)
* Zuhause (Home Setup)
* Responsive user interface

This foundation is expected to remain stable while continuing to evolve incrementally as the product grows.

---

## 🚧 Current Priority — Reliable Personal Use

The immediate focus is making Germany Move Quest dependable for everyday personal use.

Current priorities include:

* Reliable local data persistence >> COMPLETED
* Automatic restoration of application state >> COMPLETED
* Import and export of user data
* Safe reset capabilities >> COMPLETED
* Improved resilience and error handling
* Continued UX and UI refinement

The goal of this phase is to transform the application from a functional prototype into a trusted personal companion.

---

# Ongoing Product Growth

Once the core platform is dependable, investment will continue across multiple areas of the product simultaneously.

These areas are expected to evolve together rather than being completed one after another.

---

## Relocation

Continue improving support for the relocation journey.

Examples include:

* Additional quests
* Better recommendations
* Expanded relocation scenarios
* Improved onboarding
* Enhanced guidance
* Refinement based on real-world experience

---

## Settling In

Expand support for establishing a home and everyday routines.

Examples include:

* Home setup enhancements
* Utilities
* Household management
* Local administration
* Shopping
* Places and services

---

## Living Well

Continue supporting users long after the move itself.

Potential areas include:

* Seasonal experiences
* Community participation
* Local discovery
* Practical language exposure
* Lifestyle recommendations
* Leisure activities

The long-term objective is to help users feel at home in Germany rather than simply complete a relocation.

---

## Smarter Guidance

Continue improving how the application adapts to each user's circumstances.

Potential enhancements include:

* More sophisticated recommendation logic
* Quest dependencies
* Context-aware guidance
* Suggested timing
* Recurring activities
* Better prioritisation

Recommendations should become increasingly personalised while remaining transparent and understandable.

---

# Long-Term Opportunities

Future opportunities that may become appropriate as the product matures include:

* Cloud synchronisation
* Multi-device support
* User accounts
* Shared household support
* External integrations
* AI-assisted guidance

These capabilities will only be introduced when they provide meaningful value without compromising the simplicity of the product.

---

# Guiding Principles

The roadmap is guided by several long-term priorities.

* Build upon a stable foundation.
* Continue refining existing capabilities while introducing new ones.
* Prioritise real user value over feature count.
* Let genuine relocation experience inform future development.
* Preserve simplicity wherever possible.
* Support the entire transition to life in Germany—from relocation, through settling in, to living well.
