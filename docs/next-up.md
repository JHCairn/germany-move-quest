# Next Up

## Current Focus

Real-world use and observation

Germany Move Quest is now ready to support the move itself.

For the immediate future, focus on using the application during the relocation and settling-in process rather than actively expanding the product.

Use real experience to identify:

- Missing or unclear quests
- Guidance that is incomplete or difficult to use
- Resources that are particularly helpful or need improvement
- Timing or priority that does not match the real relocation experience
- Friction in the application during everyday use
- New needs that emerge while settling into Germany

Capture worthwhile ideas as they arise, but do not treat them as immediate implementation work.

---

## After That

### Places

Explore whether Germany Move Quest should allow users to save important places they discover during their journey.

Potential examples include:

- Rathaus or Bürgerbüro
- Hausarzt
- Apotheke
- Tierarzt
- Grocery stores
- Other frequently used local services

Start with the user need and data model before considering maps, navigation, automated place discovery, or other integrations.

### Living Well

Continue expanding the product beyond relocation and initial setup toward helping users feel at home in Germany.

Potential areas include:

- Local community
- Seasonal experiences
- Leisure activities
- Practical language exposure
- Local discovery
- Recurring aspects of life in Germany

## Later
